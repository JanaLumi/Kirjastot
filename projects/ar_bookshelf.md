# Augmented Reality & Spatial Access
AR Bookshelf Posters: Placing posters of bookshelves on doors or walls where specific book spines act as image-recognition targets (via QR or AR tracking).
Place-Based Digital Access: Exploring geofenced or localised access models where specific digital books or archival materials are only unlocked while physically present in or immediately around the library, re-establishing the value of physical destination for shared resources.
