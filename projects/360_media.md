# Spatial Media & 360 Production

**Immersive 360 Skits & Content:** Utilising the library's built-in curtains and room dividers to stage mini-productions, hide crew members, and test immersive storytelling formats.

**Architecture-as-Set Design:** Documenting and mapping the space's acoustic and visual layout (using the hidden kitchen and curtained alcoves) to create a repeatable framework for community media projects and live-stream experiments.
